  • New upper-wall signs fade before the top 120px header region. The original concourse sign has the same clearance behavior. Concourse camera targets lift slightly toward the TV and seating at the gate approach.
  • New fixtures include travel posters, wayfinding, a qibla arrow, real-time Toronto/Dubai/Karachi clocks, reused mechanical split-flap routes, panel and tile seams, vents, staff windows, skylight shafts, banners, a slowly turning instanced paper-plane flock, and ceiling infrastructure.   • Physical-phone frame rate remains unverified. The “about 20% blank plane” criterion was assessed visually, not by automatic segmentation.
  • No missing personal facts were filled in. No employment dates or new usage/revenue figures were added.
  • The default Turbopack compiler passed earlier builds, then encountered a cached font-import failure on the last run. The final production verification used Next’s supported Webpack compiler successfully.
